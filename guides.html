Player guides

Guarded Crate

Drop a guarded loot crate wherever you like and fight your way to it. Everyone on Beer-UK already has access — no admin needed, just chat commands.

01

What it does

Guarded Crate drops a lootable supply crate and surrounds it with armed guards. Clear the guards, the crate pops open. It's a self-made PvE fight you can start whenever you fancy a loot run.

02

How to call one in

Open chat and run /gcrate start "Medium" — that drops a Medium-tier crate and its guards at a random spot on the map, guards and all.

Want it on top of you instead? Run /gcrate here "Medium" — same thing, but right where you're standing.

Swap "Medium" for "Hard" or "Elite" for a tougher fight — see below.

03

Pick your difficulty

"Medium" — a fair fight, a handful of guards with decent gear. Good default.

"Hard" — more guards, better aim. Bring backup or good armour.

"Elite" — full squad, best gear, best loot on offer.

Easy has been retired — it was too quick to farm on repeat.

04

Before you go

Guards shoot back — they're armed and fight to the death, so go in geared up. The crate stays locked until every guard is down, no shortcuts. And guards don't check whose base it is near, so dropping one by your own walls (or anyone else's) starts a real fight there.

Questions, or found a bug?

Come ask in the Discord — happy to help.
