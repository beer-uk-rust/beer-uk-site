Server rules

Playing on Beer-UK

01

Treat people decently

No hate speech, harassment, or doxxing. Trash talk during a raid is part of Rust; targeted abuse isn't. Admins can and will act on it.

02

No cheating or exploiting

No cheat clients, ESP, aimbots, macros that give an unfair edge, or known map/mechanic exploits. This is checked and enforced — a ban here is permanent.

03

Building & bases

Build however you like — no size caps or honeycombing restrictions. Standard Rust decay rates apply.

04

Raiding

Standard rules — any base can be raided by anyone, any time, including while you're offline. There's no offline raid protection, so plan your defences accordingly.

05

Admin calls are final

Staff make judgement calls to keep the server fair and fun. Disagree respectfully via a Discord ticket rather than in chat mid-game.

06

Reporting an issue

Open a ticket in Discord with what happened, when, and any clip/screenshot you have. That's the fastest way to get it looked at.

Got a question, or want to appeal something?

Use the apply/contact form and pick the right category — it goes straight to the team.
